Natural look

Softness warms the shadow edges, relaxes the normal detail and calms the highlights. Blend lets AO and roughness settle into the lighting. Both start at 0% so the lighting stays at full strength.

Color management

Standard keeps colors as they are. Filmic rolls highlights off softly. Khronos PBR Neutral keeps base colors faithful.
